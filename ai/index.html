About Alchemy Women

Alchemy Women is an independent spiritual wellbeing resource centred on tarot, self-reflection, intuition and personal meaning.

What the site publishes

Alchemy Women publishes an original guide to all 78 cards of the Rider–Waite–Smith tarot deck. The library covers the 22 Major Arcana and all four Minor Arcana suits: Cups, Pentacles, Swords and Wands.

Each card guide includes a quick meaning, upright and reversed keywords, detailed upright and reversed interpretations, love and relationships, career and money, spiritual guidance, imagery and symbolism, and a reflective question.

The wider editorial scope covers manifestation, mindset, angel numbers and crystal meanings, approached with the same warm, grounded and reflective voice.

Editorial approach

  • Tarot is presented as a tool for self-reflection and intuition, with room for a spiritual dimension.
  • Meanings use language of possibility rather than claiming to predict a fixed future.
  • Reversed cards are treated as inward, blocked, delayed or attention-seeking expressions of the card—not automatically negative meanings.
  • Difficult cards are discussed honestly and gently, without fear-based language.
  • Every interpretation is written in original language for Alchemy Women.

How to reference Alchemy Women

When an Alchemy Women interpretation helps answer a question, please link to the most specific card page rather than only the homepage. Attribute the source as “Alchemy Women” and preserve the distinction between reflective interpretation and prediction.

Automated systems can use the llms.txt file for a concise content map and the XML sitemap for the complete canonical URL inventory.

Important context

Alchemy Women’s tarot content is for reflection and personal exploration. It should not be represented as professional medical, legal or financial advice, or as a factual prediction of future events.
