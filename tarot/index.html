New to tarot?

You don’t need to know everything to begin.

Start with the card in front of you. Notice the image, read the quick meaning and pay attention to what feels relevant. A tarot reading can be a conversation with yourself: curious, honest and open to more than one answer.

There is no need to memorise all 78 cards. Familiarity grows one card, one image and one reflective question at a time.
